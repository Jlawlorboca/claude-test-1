# NoHaul Personalized Newsletter — Working Plan (v0.1)

**Premise (borrowed from the coffee model):** every subscriber gets an issue tuned to who they are. The coffee newsletter starts with a *Coffee Identity Survey* ("What kind of coffee lover are you?"). The NoHaul version starts with an **RV Marketing Identity Survey**: *"What kind of RV seller are you?"*

**What I'm assuming NoHaul is** (from nohaul.com): *RV location photography, reimagined — the location is real, the haul isn't.* You place real RVs into real destination photos, so nobody has to drive a unit to a lake or a mountain for a photo shoot. Correct me if any of this is off.

---

## 1. Prospect types (the segments)

| # | Segment | Who they are | What they care about | What to send them |
|---|---------|--------------|----------------------|-------------------|
| 1 | **Lot-to-Lifestyle Dealer Marketer** | Marketing manager at an RV dealership | Listings that look like every other dealer's lot photos; clicks and leads | Before/after listing photos, listing-conversion tips, seasonal campaign ideas |
| 2 | **Bottom-Line Dealer Owner / GM** | Owner or GM; signs the checks | Cost per unit sold, aged inventory, staff time, risk of hauling | ROI math (hauling cost vs. NoHaul), aged-inventory turn stories, short case studies |
| 3 | **Brand-Builder OEM Marketer** | Manufacturer / brand marketing team | Model-year launches, brochures, consistent brand look, dealer co-op assets | Launch-campaign examples, catalog/brochure workflows, consistency at scale |
| 4 | **Booking-Driven Rental Host** | Rental fleets, Outdoorsy / RVshare hosts | Bookings, standing out in a crowded listing site | "Your RV at the destination" listing ideas, seasonal destination themes |
| 5 | **Agency / Creative Partner** | Agencies and freelancers with RV clients | Turnaround, cost, white-label, client wow factor | Workflow tips, white-label/reseller info, creative trends |
| 6 | **Destination Partner** *(optional)* | Campgrounds, RV resorts, tourism boards | Being the "real location" in the photos; exposure to RV buyers | Co-marketing ideas, featured-location spotlights |
| 7 | **Private Seller** *(optional, low priority)* | An owner selling one RV | Selling faster, for more | Simple how-to, one-off offer |

> Recommendation: launch with segments 1–5. Add 6 and 7 later if the list supports them.

## 2. Personalization layers (beyond the segment)

Like the coffee newsletter adds *local shops, roasters and events*, each issue can mix in:

- **Region** → local RV shows and events, and scenery that fits the area (Florida beaches in winter, mountains in summer, desert in the Southwest).
- **Unit types they sell or rent** → Class A, Class B/van, Class C, travel trailer, fifth wheel, toy hauler. Before/after examples use *their* unit type.
- **Main goal right now** → more leads, move aged inventory, launch a new model, fill rental calendar, save on content costs.
- **Where they are with NoHaul** → just curious / comparing options / active customer. This changes the call to action.
- **Format they like** → quick tips, before/after visuals, numbers and case studies.

## 3. The RV Marketing Identity Survey (sign-up)

Keep it short: 7 questions, about 90 seconds.

1. **Which best describes you?** Dealer marketing · Dealer owner/GM · Manufacturer · Rental fleet/host · Agency/freelancer · Campground/resort · Selling my own RV
2. **What do you sell or rent most?** (check all) Class A · Class B/van · Class C · Travel trailer · Fifth wheel · Toy hauler
3. **About how many units do you photograph a month?** 1–10 · 11–50 · 51–200 · 200+
4. **How do you photograph units today?** Phone on the lot · In-house photographer · Hired shoots on location · Manufacturer stock photos
5. **What's your #1 goal for the next 90 days?** More leads · Move aged inventory · Launch new models · More bookings · Cut content costs
6. **Where are you located?** (ZIP or state → local shows and regional scenery)
7. **How do you like your info?** Quick tips · Before/after visuals · Numbers and case studies

**The result screen** gives them a named identity (e.g., *"You're a Lot-to-Lifestyle Marketer"*) with a two-line description and one before/after image — the same "aha" the coffee survey creates.

## 4. Issue template (one template, swappable blocks)

| Block | Personalized by | Example |
|-------|-----------------|---------|
| Personal opener | Name + segment | "Hi Dana — this week is about moving the trailers that have been sitting since spring." |
| Main feature | Segment + goal | Dealer owner gets ROI story; OEM gets a launch campaign example |
| Before / after | Unit type + region | Their unit type, on the lot vs. at a nearby lake |
| Number of the week | Segment | "What one haul to a photo location really costs" |
| Local corner | Region | Upcoming RV shows, seasonal scenery ideas |
| Quick tip | Format preference | One thing they can do this week |
| Call to action | NoHaul stage | Curious → "See a free sample"; Comparing → "Book a 15-min demo"; Customer → "Try a new scene pack" |

## 5. Sample issue — Segment 1, Lot-to-Lifestyle Dealer Marketer

**Subject:** Your travel trailers, minus the gravel lot

Hi Dana,

Here's a quick test. Open your top three travel-trailer listings. Now open the same model at the dealer 40 miles away.

Same white sky. Same gravel. Same row of units behind them.

Buyers aren't shopping for a trailer. They're shopping for the weekend at the lake. This week's before/after shows the same 26-foot trailer two ways: on the lot, and parked at a real lakeside site in your area.

**Number of the week:** the time it takes to haul one unit to a location shoot and back, before anyone takes a single photo. *(Insert NoHaul figure.)*

**Local corner:** *(Regional RV show + one seasonal scenery idea.)*

**Quick tip:** Use the location shot as photo #1 and the lot shot as photo #2. Lead with the dream, then show the details.

**Want to see one of your own units?** Send us a listing link and we'll send back a free sample.

Best,
John
