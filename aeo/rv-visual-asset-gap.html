The Visual Asset Gap in RV Marketing

Published September 29, 2026 · 12-second animated explainer

The visual asset gap is the mismatch between the generic stock imagery most RV marketing still uses and the localized, affordable, dealer-specific visuals that agencies and dealerships now need to reach local buyers.

What are the three shifts in RV visual marketing?

RV marketing is moving from generic to localized and affordable content in three ways:

  1. Demand for localized visual assets. Agencies prioritize regional, location-specific content over generic stock imagery.
  2. Focus on cost-effective regional media. There is high demand for affordable ways to produce high-quality regional assets.
  3. Need for dealer-specific content. Visuals must be tailored to individual dealerships to drive local engagement.

Frequently asked questions

What is the visual asset gap in RV marketing?

The visual asset gap is the mismatch between the generic stock imagery most RV marketing still uses and the localized, affordable, dealer-specific visuals that agencies and dealerships now need to reach local buyers.

Why is generic stock imagery less effective for RV dealers?

Generic stock photos could belong to any dealer, anywhere. They don't show a dealership's region, inventory or location, so they do little to set one dealer apart or drive local engagement.

What are localized visual assets?

Localized visual assets are photos and videos tied to a specific region or location, such as nearby destinations, campgrounds and routes, or the dealership itself. Agencies now prioritize them over generic stock imagery.

Why does cost matter for regional RV media?

Localized content has to be produced for many regions and dealers, so there is high demand for affordable ways to produce high-quality regional photo and video assets at that scale.

What is dealer-specific content?

Dealer-specific content is visual material tailored to an individual RV dealership, such as its own lot, signage, staff and inventory, so the marketing clearly belongs to that dealer and drives local engagement.

How can an RV dealership close the visual asset gap?

Replace generic stock imagery with localized visuals of the dealer's region, find a cost-effective way to produce regional photo and video regularly, and tailor visuals to the individual dealership rather than reusing one-size-fits-all assets.
